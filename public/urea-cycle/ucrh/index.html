About the Urea Cycle

The urea cycle is the primary pathway for ammonia detoxification in humans, converting toxic nitrogen that is produced from amino acid breakdown into urea, and serves as the principal mechanism for the clearance of waste nitrogen from protein and other nitrogenous compounds. Deficiencies in the Urea Cycle's enzymes lead to several urea cycle disorders (UCDs), and these can be grouped as metabolic errors characterized by hyperammonemia and neurological impairment.
