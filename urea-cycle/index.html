The Urea Cycle Research Hub

Automated literature curation and multi-parameter variant analysis across eight disease genes

Karanveer Sadiora, with Ljubica Caldovic, PhD and Hiroki Morizono, PhD
Center for Precision Medicine and Genomics Research, Children's National Research Institute

A web platform that pulls urea cycle literature from PubMed and catalogues 2,341 missense variants across eight genes, scored with AlphaMissense, DynaMut2, REVEL and ClinVar and shown on 3D protein structures.
