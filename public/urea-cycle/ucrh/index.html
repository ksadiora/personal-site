Background

The urea cycle is how the liver clears ammonia: nitrogen released by amino acid breakdown is converted to urea and excreted. A deficiency in any of its enzymes or transporters causes a urea cycle disorder, in which ammonia builds up in the blood and can damage the brain.

The site checks PubMed for new papers on each gene. Results can be exported as RIS or a list of PMIDs.
